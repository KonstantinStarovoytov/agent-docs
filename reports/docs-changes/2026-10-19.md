## Changed significantly (8)
- **cursor › Get Started** — <https://cursor.com/docs/grok-bot/get-started.md>
  Bot creation now uses **New Bot** or the command palette for Team Bots, and the guide refers to connected apps instead of plugins.
  _13 lines_
- **cursor › Work** — <https://cursor.com/docs/grok-bot/work.md>
  Bots now use connected apps instead of plugins, and Team Bot managers can edit descriptions; sharing and @-mention guidance was updated accordingly.
  _+ Connect apps, − Connect plugins, 24 lines_
- **cursor › Settings** — <https://cursor.com/docs/grok-bot/settings.md>
  Plugins are now called apps, and admins may restrict which apps team members can use.
  _+ Connected apps, − Plugins, 4 lines_
- **cursor › Teams** — <https://cursor.com/docs/grok-bot/teams.md>
  Team Bots can now be shared with selected people or groups, with admins managing access and auto-add settings.
  _+ Why does a member see an app as Disabled by team admin?, − Why does a member see a plugin as Disabled by team admin?, 20 lines_
- **cursor › Identity** — <https://cursor.com/docs/grok-bot/identity.md>
  The page now refers to connected-app sign-in instead of plugin sign-in and clarifies that connecting an app doesn’t go through the computer.
  _+ Does the device-trust change affect connected-app sign-in?, − Does the device-trust change affect plugin sign-in?, 10 lines_
- **cursor › Security Faq** — <https://cursor.com/docs/grok-bot/security-faq.md>
  The FAQ now refers to connected apps and app calls instead of plugins and plugin calls.
  _+ If we block an app, can a Bot still reach that service in the browser?, − If we block a plugin, can a Bot still reach that service in the browser?, 8 lines_
- **cursor › Plugins** — <https://cursor.com/docs/plugins.md>
  Team admins can now add plugins from their Marketplace pages to team marketplaces and set their installation defaults.
  _+ Add a plugin from its Marketplace page, 12 lines_
- **cursor › Bugbot** — <https://cursor.com/docs/bugbot.md>
  Bugbot now skips credential-looking files but still reviews templates and other files, including source code with hardcoded keys.
  _+ Does Bugbot read credential files?, 4 lines_

Minor edits: 14 pages.
